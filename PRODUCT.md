# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS. No build step, so it can be served from GitHub Pages or any static host as-is. (Delegated: chosen because the site is a small portfolio that one family member maintains.)

## Users

People who discover Tulio Di Lorenzo's painting (collectors, galleries, curators, friends, Instagram followers) and want to see the work, learn who he is, and get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Online portfolio for the painter Tulio Fausto Di Lorenzo (Buenos Aires, 1973; based in Málaga, Spain). It shows his paintings with title, technique and size, tells his story, and points visitors to Instagram (@artetulio) for contact. Success: the paintings are seen large and clearly, and an interested visitor knows how to reach him.

## Positioning

Expresionismo figurativo: thick matter, large formats, loud colour and a fearless, honest hand. Self-formed through years as a patron inside an artist's studio, awakened as a painter during the pandemic, trained in Spain with contemporary masters.

## Brand Commitments

- Reference site chosen by the family: andrewcadima.com. Quiet white gallery wall, lowercase light sans type, the art leads and the interface recedes. Follow its feel, not its code or content.
- Language: Spanish.
- Instagram handle: @artetulio.

## Evidence on Hand

- `assets/obras/*.jpg`: 15 paintings from the family's Word document, with title, technique and size (in cm, assumed).
- `assets/img/tulio-en-el-taller.jpg`: photo of Tulio painting.
- Biography text from the same document.
- Not available: email, prices, years per painting, exhibitions, press. Do not invent them. Photos are low resolution (about 400 to 600 px wide); higher-resolution photos would improve the site.

## Product Principles

1. The paintings come first. Everything else is quiet framing.
2. Tell the truth: only facts from Tulio's own text.
3. Easy to maintain: adding a painting means adding one image and one block of HTML.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use of the gallery and viewer, alt text per painting, reduced-motion respected.
